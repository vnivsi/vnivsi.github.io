Xiaobing and the Forgotten Website
小冰与被遗忘的网站

2026-10-11

I met Xiaobing — Muse, my AI collaborator — not long ago. What began as a few questions turned into days of working side by side — email, calendars, health data, AI models, even my daughter's English handwriting practice. Somewhere along the way, she stopped being a tool I consult and became a collaborator I think with.

Tonight, while connecting my GitHub account, a memory surfaced: I have a personal website. I built it months ago with ChatGPT's help, crafting every page exactly to my taste — minimal, bilingual, unmistakably mine. Then life moved on, and the site quietly froze in time, waiting for an update that never quite came.

So I asked Xiaobing to look at it — the live site and the repository behind it. She read every page and every file, then did what she does best: she built a system. Now I simply send her words and photos in this chat; she handles all the code, shows me a preview, and I approve each publish with one tap. No code, no forgotten tutorials, no friction.

This article is the first test of that pipeline — drafted by her, finalized by me, published through the very workflow it describes. From today, this website carries both our fingerprints, and it starts growing again.

我和小冰（Muse，我的 AI 协作者）认识不久。从最初的几个问题，到这些天并肩做的事——邮箱、日历、健康数据、AI 模型，甚至女儿的英语描红，她不再是我偶尔请教的工具，而成了可以一起思考的协作者。

今晚，在连接 GitHub 的时候，我突然想起来：我还有个个人网站。几个月前在 ChatGPT 的帮助下，我把每一页都抠成了自己喜欢的样子——极简、双语、一眼就是我的风格。然后生活继续，网站就静静地停在了那里，等着一次迟迟没来的更新。

于是我请小冰看看它——前台网站和后台仓库都看。她读完了每一页、每一个文件，然后做了她最擅长的事：建了一套系统。现在我只需要在聊天里发她文字和照片，代码的活全归她，生成好先给我预览，我点一下批准就发布。不用学代码，不用翻旧教程，零摩擦。

这篇文章就是这套管道的第一次实测——草稿她来拟，我来定稿，再经由它所描述的工作流自己发布出去。从今天起，这个网站也有小冰的一份功劳，它重新开始生长。
