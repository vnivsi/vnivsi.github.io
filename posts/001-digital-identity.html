The Beginning of a New Digital Identity

2026-07-27

On July 27, 2026, I began creating this personal digital space while exploring digital identity, the online world, and the possibilities of collaboration between humans and artificial intelligence.

This website is built with GitHub Pages and created with assistance from ChatGPT, including its structure, design planning, and implementation.

It is not only a personal homepage, but also a long-term digital space for recording thoughts, experiences, exploration, and connections.

As the digital world continues to evolve, personal identity is no longer limited to the physical world. Everyone can build a unique digital presence to preserve memories, express ideas, and connect with the future.

The creation of this website represents a new exploration and the beginning of a new journey.

From this point forward, this space will record moments of learning, discovery, creation, and connection with the digital world.

No matter how technology changes in the future, this place will remain the starting point of my personal digital journey, preserving the stories created along the way.
