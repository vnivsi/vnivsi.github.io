Hello, I'm Liam.

I am a filmmaker, director, musician, and explorer of the digital world.

My journey began with storytelling and visual creation, and gradually expanded into music, technology, and the endless possibilities of the Internet.

Through years of exploration and creation, I have participated in different forms of artistic expression and established my presence across multiple platforms and professional communities both locally and internationally.

This personal digital space is where I record thoughts, preserve moments, and explore the connection between creativity, technology, and people.

我的旅程始于故事与影像创作，并逐渐延伸至音乐、技术，以及充满可能性的互联网世界。
